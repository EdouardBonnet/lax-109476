# Fourier–Motzkin proof attribution

`Lax109476Proofs/Elimination/` adapts the algebra helpers, elimination, and
Farkas proof from [Jyotirmoy Bhattacharya, farkas_lean](https://github.com/jmoy/farkas_lean),
revision `30c319dd52ca89cfa82a68352b1f39f4f3026fc2`.
The original MIT license is reproduced at the head of each adapted file.
The adapter specializes the Farkas proof to real coefficients and makes its
elimination assumption an explicit Lax concept.
